thinking about why we switched to airless pumps. first batch of the serum went out in dropper bottles in 2023 and during that monsoon we got a cluster of returns from Kerala and Mumbai saying it had gone darker and smelled off. when we pulled retained samples from the same batch that had stayed sealed in the warehouse they were fine. so it wasn't the formula, it was every time someone opened the dropper and let humid air in. airless cost us more per unit and I resisted it for months. I want to say packaging is part of the formulation, not an afterthought, and people should ask how a product is protected after it's opened, not just what's in it
