ok so the thing with vitamin C serums, L-ascorbic acid, people keep asking why we don't sell one. honestly it's because the stuff oxidises so fast once it's opened, especially in Mumbai humidity, and most brands don't publish any stability data past the unopened shelf life. we tested a couple of market samples in the lab and by week six they'd gone that orange colour. I want to say something about asking brands for opened-bottle stability data, not just the expiry date on the box.
