niacinamide thing I keep coming back to. everyone's selling 10% now like higher is better. when we were reformulating last year we trialled 10% against 4% on the same base and the 10% batch got noticeably more complaints about flushing and tingling in the first two weeks, and honestly the results people reported weren't better. most of the published work I've read on barrier and redness uses somewhere in the 2 to 5% range. I want to say the percentage on the front is a marketing choice as much as a formulation one, and ask brands what concentration their efficacy claims were actually tested at
